## Journey Overview

### Key Personas

**Persona 1: Community Leader (Primary)**
- Role: Mosque imam, cultural association president, community organizer
- Age: 35-65
- Tech Savviness: Medium (can use WhatsApp, Facebook, email)
- Pain Points: Facebook Groups chaos, WhatsApp overwhelm, lost photos
- Goals: Organize community, preserve cultural heritage, engage members

**Persona 2: Community Member (Secondary)**
- Role: Family representative, active participant
- Age: 25-75 (multi-generational)
- Tech Savviness: Low-Medium (grandparents to parents to youth)
- Pain Points: Can't find event photos, missed announcements, too many WhatsApp notifications
- Goals: Stay connected, RSVP to events, view/share photos

## Community Leader Journey

### Stage 1: Awareness (Week 0)

**Scenario:**
Ahmed is an imam at a mosque in Chicago. His community uses a Facebook Group (150 members) and a WhatsApp group (80 members) to coordinate Friday prayers, Eid celebrations, and community events.

**Trigger:**
- An important announcement (funeral, emergency fundraiser) gets buried in Facebook's algorithmic feed
- Families complain they can't find photos from the recent Eid celebration
- WhatsApp group is chaotic (80 members sending 100+ messages per day)

**How Ahmed Discovers Tribe:**
- Receives LinkedIn message from Tribe founder
- OR: Sees Tribe demo at inter-mosque conference
- OR: Referred by another mosque leader using Tribe

**Touchpoints:**
- LinkedIn outreach message
- Landing page (diaspora-specific)
- Demo video (3 minutes)

**Emotions:**
- 😫 Frustrated with current tools (Facebook, WhatsApp)
- 🤔 Skeptical (another tool? will my community adopt?)
- 🤞 Hopeful (maybe this solves our problems)

**Success Criteria:**
- Ahmed clicks through to landing page
- Ahmed books discovery call

---

### Stage 2: Evaluation (Week 0)

**Scenario:**
Ahmed books a 15-minute discovery call to learn more about Tribe.

**Touchpoints:**
- Discovery call (15 min) with Tribe founder
- Demo call (30 min) - live walkthrough
- Pilot agreement (email)

**Discovery Call (15 min):**
- Tribe founder asks about current pain points
- Ahmed explains: Facebook feed buries announcements, WhatsApp is chaotic, Eid photos are lost
- Tribe founder shows mockups of The Vault (organized albums), RBAC (elder/family/youth permissions), Events (RSVP)

**Demo Call (30 min):**
- Live demo of Tribe
- Walk through tribe creation, album upload, event creation, permission settings
- Q&A: "Can we migrate from Facebook?" "Is it free?" "What about privacy?"

**Emotions:**
- 😮 Impressed (The Vault solves photo problem)
- 🤔 Concerned (will seniors be able to use this?)
- 😊 Relieved (finally a solution for our community)

**Decision Point:**
- Does Ahmed see enough value to pilot Tribe with his mosque?

**Success Criteria:**
- Ahmed signs pilot agreement
- Ahmed sends list of 30 initial members (names + emails)

---

### Stage 3: Onboarding (Week 1-2)

**Scenario:**
Ahmed has signed up for the pilot. Now he needs to set up the tribe and invite members.

**Touchpoints:**
- Onboarding call (1 hour) with Tribe founder (screen share)
- Tribe creation wizard (5 steps)
- First album upload (recent Eid celebration photos)
- Member invitations (30 emails sent)

**Onboarding Call (1 hour):**
1. Create tribe: "Chicago Mosque Community"
2. Set up permissions:
   - Elders (admin) - can create events, moderate posts
   - Families (standard) - can post, upload photos, RSVP
   - Youth (view-only) - can view, RSVP, but can't post
3. Upload first album: "Eid al-Fitr 2024" (15 photos)
4. Invite first 30 members (copy/paste emails from WhatsApp group)

**Welcome Email to Members:**
```
Subject: Join Our Mosque Community on Tribe

As-salamu alaykum,

Imam Ahmed has created a private community for our mosque on Tribe,
a new platform to help us organize events and preserve our memories.

You can:
- View photos from Eid, Ramadan, and community events
- RSVP to upcoming events (Friday prayers, fundraisers)
- Stay updated with announcements

Click here to join: [Invitation Link]

JazakAllah khair,
Imam Ahmed
```

**Emotions:**
- 😰 Anxious (will this work? will members join?)
- 🤓 Learning (new platform, need to get familiar)
- 😊 Excited (this could really help our community)

**Friction Points:**
- RBAC setup is confusing (too many permission options)
- Photo upload takes time (bulk upload from phone)
- Members don't know what Tribe is (need to explain)

**Success Criteria:**
- Tribe created successfully
- 30 invitations sent
- First album uploaded (Eid photos)
- 60%+ members accept invitations within 1 week

---

### Stage 4: Activation (Week 3-4)

**Scenario:**
Invitations have been sent. Now Ahmed needs to ensure members activate and engage.

**Touchpoints:**
- Weekly check-in call (15 min) with Tribe founder
- Member activation reminders (email)
- First event created (upcoming Friday prayer)
- Community gathering (Ahmed demos Tribe in-person)

**Week 3 Check-In Call:**
- Tribe founder: "How's it going? How many members have joined?"
- Ahmed: "18 out of 30 have joined (60%). Some seniors need help."
- Tribe founder: "Great! Let's troubleshoot. Can you show me the RBAC settings?"

**Ahmed's Actions:**
- Sends reminder emails to members who haven't joined
- Creates first event: "Friday Prayer + Community Dinner" (next week)
- Demos Tribe at Friday prayer gathering: "Check out our Eid photos on Tribe!"
- Helps seniors activate (walks them through signup on mobile)

**Emotions:**
- 😅 Relieved (members are joining!)
- 🤔 Concerned (some seniors struggling with signup)
- 😊 Proud (community is engaging, viewing Eid photos)

**Friction Points:**
- Seniors struggle with email verification (don't check email regularly)
- Members don't understand RSVP (how to respond to events)
- Ahmed spends time answering "How do I upload photos?" questions

**Success Criteria:**
- 60%+ members activated (accepted invite + uploaded profile pic)
- First event created (upcoming Friday prayer)
- 10+ members RSVP to event
- Members viewing Eid album (engagement)

---

### Stage 5: Retention (Month 2-3)

**Scenario:**
The pilot is live. Now Ahmed needs to ensure sustained engagement and build photo lock-in.

**Touchpoints:**
- Bi-weekly check-in calls
- Second cultural event (Ramadan iftar)
- Second album created ("Ramadan Iftar 2024")
- Community members uploading photos proactively

**Ramadan Iftar Event:**
- Ahmed creates event on Tribe: "Ramadan Iftar - March 15"
- 40 members RSVP (going)
- Event happens (in-person gathering)
- Ahmed uploads 20 photos to album: "Ramadan Iftar 2024"
- 5 families also upload photos (community contributing)

**Photo Lock-In Moment:**
- Grandparent: "Can I see the Eid photos from last year?"
- Family member: "They're on Tribe! Check the 'Eid al-Fitr 2024' album."
- Grandparent browses album, shares with family
- **Lock-in achieved:** Cultural heritage preserved, irreplaceable memories

**Ahmed's Realization:**
- "We've uploaded 50+ photos from 3 events (Eid, Iftar, community gathering)"
- "Families are viewing and sharing albums with relatives"
- "We can't go back to Facebook - our memories are here"

**Emotions:**
- 😊 Satisfied (Tribe is working, community is engaged)
- 🤝 Grateful (Tribe solved our photo problem)
- 🔒 Locked In (we can't leave, our heritage is here)

**Success Criteria:**
- 3-5 albums created (Eid, Ramadan, community events)
- 50-100+ photos uploaded
- Members viewing albums (high engagement)
- Ahmed NPS 9-10 at 90 days
- Community begins discussing full migration from Facebook

---

### Stage 6: Advocacy (Month 3-6)

**Scenario:**
Ahmed is now a Tribe champion. He refers other mosque leaders and provides testimonials.

**Touchpoints:**
- Referral to other mosque leaders (word-of-mouth)
- Testimonial for case study
- Featured on Tribe landing page

**Word-of-Mouth Moment:**
- Ahmed attends inter-mosque conference
- Mosque leader from NYC: "How do you organize your community?"
- Ahmed: "We use Tribe. It's been a game-changer. Want me to intro you to the founder?"
- Referral sent → NYC mosque signs up for pilot

**Testimonial:**
```
"Tribe has transformed how our mosque community stays connected. We used
to lose photos from Eid celebrations and Ramadan iftars. Now, everything
is organized in albums that families can view and share. The permission
settings are perfect - elders can moderate, youth can view but not post.
We can't imagine going back to Facebook Groups."

- Imam Ahmed, Chicago Mosque Community
```

**Emotions:**
- 🙌 Advocacy (telling everyone about Tribe)
- 🤝 Partnership (feels like a co-creator)
- 😊 Pride (our community is thriving)

**Success Criteria:**
- 2-3 referrals to other mosque leaders
- Testimonial provided
- Case study published

## Community Member Journey

### Stage 1: Invitation (Week 1)

**Scenario:**
Fatima (age 45, family representative) receives an email invitation to join the mosque's Tribe community.

**Touchpoints:**
- Email invitation from Imam Ahmed
- Tribe signup page

**Email:**
```
Subject: Join Our Mosque Community on Tribe

[See earlier example]
```

**Fatima's Reaction:**
- "What's Tribe? Is this another app?"
- Clicks invitation link (curious)
- Sees signup page: "Create your account"

**Emotions:**
- 🤔 Confused (what is this?)
- 🤷 Skeptical (do I need another app?)
- 🤞 Trusting (Imam Ahmed sent it, so it's safe)

**Success Criteria:**
- Fatima clicks invitation link
- Fatima creates account (accepts invite)

---

### Stage 2: Activation (Week 1-2)

**Scenario:**
Fatima signs up and explores the tribe.

**Touchpoints:**
- Account creation (email + password)
- Profile setup (upload profile pic)
- Browse first album ("Eid al-Fitr 2024")

**Fatima's Experience:**
1. Creates account (email: fatima@gmail.com, password)
2. Email verification: "Please verify your email"
3. Clicks verification link
4. Prompted to upload profile pic (skips for now)
5. Lands on tribe home page
6. Sees album: "Eid al-Fitr 2024" (15 photos)
7. Clicks album, browses photos
8. Finds photo of her family at Eid prayer
9. **Aha moment:** "This is amazing! I can finally see all our Eid photos in one place!"

**Emotions:**
- 😊 Delighted (sees Eid photos)
- 🤩 Impressed (organized albums)
- 🤝 Connected (feels part of community)

**Success Criteria:**
- Fatima uploads profile pic
- Fatima views album (engagement)
- Fatima returns to Tribe within 7 days

---

### Stage 3: Engagement (Week 3-4)

**Scenario:**
Fatima engages with the tribe (RSVP to events, upload photos).

**Touchpoints:**
- Event RSVP (upcoming Friday prayer)
- Photo upload (uploads family photo)
- Comment on post (Ramadan preparation discussion)

**Event RSVP:**
- Sees event: "Friday Prayer + Community Dinner"
- Clicks "Going"
- Receives confirmation: "You're attending!"

**Photo Upload:**
- Fatima uploads photo of her family from Eid (from her phone)
- Adds to "Eid al-Fitr 2024" album
- Other families see it, react with likes

**Emotions:**
- 😊 Contributing (uploaded photo)
- 🤝 Connected (participating in community)
- 🔔 Engaged (checking Tribe daily)

**Success Criteria:**
- Fatima RSVPs to event
- Fatima uploads photo
- Fatima comments on post

---

### Stage 4: Habit Formation (Month 2-3)

**Scenario:**
Fatima now checks Tribe regularly (weekly habit).

**Touchpoints:**
- Weekly visit to view new photos
- RSVP to multiple events (Ramadan iftar, community gathering)
- Shares Tribe with other family members

**Habit Loop:**
1. Ramadan iftar event happens (in-person)
2. Fatima checks Tribe next day: "Are the photos up yet?"
3. Sees album: "Ramadan Iftar 2024" (20 photos)
4. Browses photos, finds family photo
5. Shares with husband: "Look, we're in the Ramadan album!"
6. Husband joins Tribe to view photos

**Emotions:**
- 🔁 Habitual (checks Tribe weekly)
- 🤝 Connected (feels part of community)
- 📸 Nostalgic (views old event photos)

**Success Criteria:**
- Fatima visits Tribe weekly (retention)
- Fatima invites family members (network effect)
- Fatima engaged with 3+ events

## Pain Points & Friction

### Community Leader Pain Points

| Stage | Pain Point | Friction | Solution |
|-------|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Evaluation** | "Will my community adopt?" | Skepticism | Demo shows easy UX, pilot is low-risk |
| **Onboarding** | RBAC setup is confusing | Too many permission options | Role templates (Elder, Family, Youth) |
| **Activation** | Seniors struggle with signup | Email verification, password setup | In-person help at community gathering |
| **Retention** | Members not uploading photos | Requires proactive action | Email prompts after events |

---

### Community Member Pain Points

| Stage | Pain Point | Friction | Solution |
|-------|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Invitation** | "What's Tribe?" | Unfamiliar platform | Clear invitation email from trusted leader |
| **Activation** | Email verification | Check email, click link | Simplify verification (optional skip for low-risk users) |
| **Engagement** | "How do I upload photos?" | Not intuitive | In-app tutorial, help docs |
| **Habit** | Forget to check Tribe | No push notifications (Phase 1) | Weekly email digest (upcoming events, new photos) |
